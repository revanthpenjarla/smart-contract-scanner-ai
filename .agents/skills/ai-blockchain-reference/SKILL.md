## Answering questions

When answering questions about the paper:

1. Prefer the paper reference over general knowledge.
2. Preserve the paper's terminology and claims.
3. Include relevant numerical values.
4. Distinguish what the paper explicitly states from inference.
5. If the paper does not provide an answer, say so.
6. If different sections report different values, report the
   discrepancy rather than silently choosing one.
